## What's new

- **Works offline:** with no connection, OpenGallery keeps rotating through artwork it has already downloaded, following your filters where it can. The menu shows when it's offline.
- **Smaller cache:** keeps the 15 most recent images instead of 30, about 25–50 MB depending on your screens.
- **Verifiable download:** GitHub now builds each release from the tagged source and signs a record of it. See Verify below to check your copy.
