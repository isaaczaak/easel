## What's new

- **3× the collection:** about 60,000 works, up from 20,000. Portrait and square works now hang whole on a gallery wall instead of being cropped.
- **Orientation filter:** landscape only, portrait & square only, or both. Settings now shows how many artworks match your filters.
- **Endless swiping:** swipe back past your history for fresh artwork. Swipes are smoother, with no flicker, and show a spinner while the next artwork downloads.
- **Smoother on/off:** switching OpenGallery on or off fades between wallpapers.
- **Lighter:** uses about 50 MB of memory.
