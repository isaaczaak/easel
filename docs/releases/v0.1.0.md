## What's new

First release of OpenGallery.

- **Your desktop as a gallery:** about 20,000 public-domain works from the National Gallery of Art, changing every 15 minutes, hour, 3 hours or day.
- **Each display its own art,** or the same artwork everywhere.
- **Filters:** type, art movement and palette. Nudity is hidden by default.
- **Desktop gestures:** swipe sideways with two fingers to change the artwork; Force Click to see its title, artist, date and medium.
- **Favorites:** keep up to 20, and show only those if you like.
- **On/off switch** in the menu that brings back your old wallpaper.
